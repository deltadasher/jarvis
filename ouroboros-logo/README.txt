OUROBOROS / CLOSED ORBIT

Recommended direction: a single tapered serpent loop with a clearly engaged tail. The head and negative eye recall the reference category; the orbital clutter is removed. All geometry is newly drawn, with no traced or embedded source assets.

USAGE
Use the primary master at 32 px and above; use the micro master below 32 px. For clearly visible snake and bite detail, prefer 48 px or larger. At 16–24 px the ring silhouette is prioritized over anatomical detail. SVGs are actual filled vector paths, not raster images. The primary and micro marks contain no fonts, embedded images or linked assets. Inline SVG inherits CSS color; for external images, set the fill value to your desired color. Keep approximately one body-width of clear space around the visible mark. Do not compress or stretch nonuniformly.

The proposed name and color are optional. No trademark clearance is implied.
